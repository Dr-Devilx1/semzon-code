Case study · Sindh

Fertilizer-scale fabrication.

A BOP fertilizer plant for Auriga, Karachi — SEMZON's standing reference for fertilizer-scale process fabrication delivered turnkey.

BOP plant50–200 t/dKarachi

Auriga required a bone-based (BOP) fertilizer plant at process scale — grinding, granulation, drying and screening engineered to run as one continuous line rather than a set of separate machines.

SEMZON delivered the full process chain with materials handling and structures fabricated in-house, coordinating every transfer point, height and airflow before anything shipped.

The result anchors the SEMZON fertilizer reference list and underpins the SSP and BOP plant scopes offered today.

Scope delivered

  • Full BOP process line
  • Granulation, drying & screening
  • In-house structures & handling
  • Coordinated process fabrication
  • Commissioning to rated output
  • Ongoing support from Lahore
