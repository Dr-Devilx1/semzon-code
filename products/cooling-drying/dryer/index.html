Belt Dryer

Multi-layer belt drying that takes extruded feed down to safe moisture gently and evenly — the partner machine to every extruder.

Extruded aqua feedStage 05 · Drying

The SHGW belt dryer carries extruded pellets across stacked mesh belts through controlled hot-air zones, drying to target moisture without case-hardening or breaking the product.

Steam and gas heating options match the utilities available at your site.

Why plants specify it

  • Multi-layer belt design, even residence time
  • Zone-controlled hot air
  • Steam or gas heating options
  • Gentle handling of expanded product
